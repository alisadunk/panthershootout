## Coach and Competition Essentials

The [fillable game-card PDF](docs/28%20PSO%20Game%20Card.pdf) has 46 shared fields for division, team ID, team name/colors, coaches, and the 20 existing player-name/number rows. Entries repeat across all three cards on the first page using native PDF form fields, without JavaScript. The printed roster capacity does not change the division roster limits above. Scores, playing time, and the referee sections remain for handwriting. The supplied file is blank; sample entries used for verification are not included.

Based on the Tournament Rules, with match lengths cross-checked against the Referee Plan:

- **Check in 60 minutes before the first game.** Bring exactly five completed game cards printed on card stock using the tournament's fillable PDF template, matching the approved roster in jersey-number order. One card is used to track shootout participation.
- Bring AYSO Player Registration forms with original ink or electronic signatures and keep them available throughout the event. Players need not attend coach check-in; late arrivals must be checked in by staff before playing.
- **Every player must play at least half of each game.** Substitutions occur approximately midway through each half and are recorded by the referee.
- Pool format and advancement depend on division size. The Rules describe four-, five-, six-, and eight-team formats; confirm the final bracket before scheduling.
- Pool games use a running clock, a five-minute halftime, and no added time; ties are allowed. Tied final matches go directly to kicks from the penalty mark, with **no overtime**.
- First- and second-place finalists receive medals; consolation participants do not. Each player and coach receives a tournament pin.

| Division | Pool play / semifinals | Final rounds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10U | 24-minute halves | 25-minute halves |
| 12U | 25-minute halves | 30-minute halves |
| 14U | 25-minute halves | 35-minute halves |

The five-team round-robin clause separately says “All games are 25 min”; confirm whether this means halves and which divisions it overrides.

### Panther Shootout Competition

This is scored **separately from match standings**. After each of the three preliminary games, each team supplies five shooters in the fixed order submitted on the white PK lineup card at initial check-in. Every player shoots at least once; teams with fewer than 15 players cycle back through the order. Winners are determined by total shootout goals, then PK goals allowed, then a sudden-victory shootout.

### Standings and Conduct

Pool standings use six points for a win, three for a tie, up to three goal points, and one shutout point. A forfeit earns seven points. Yellow cards deduct one point and red cards deduct two, including the player/substitute/coach cases specified in the Rules. Consult the Rules for the full standings tie-break sequence.

Coaches are responsible for team and spectator conduct. Referees record misconduct and return game cards to Field Monitors; monitors also coordinate incident and first-aid response by radio. The Rules place first aid at the main tent and provide for a roving responder. Written protests are limited to player eligibility or required playing time and must reach the Tournament Director within **30 minutes after the match**; referee judgment calls cannot be protested.

## Referee Requirements

The Referee Plan asks each team to provide **three or more qualified referees**. The **$270 deposit** is fully refundable for nine completed assignments and prorated at **$30 per completed assignment** below nine. Standby assignments earn equal credit when check-in requirements are met; standby referees may serve as Field Monitors.

- Referees must be currently registered AYSO volunteers with current certifications and may represent only one tournament team.
- Minimum badge levels: **Regional for 10U, Intermediate for 12U, Advanced for 14U**. Exceptions require prior written approval from the tournament Referee Administrator.
- The Referee Plan prohibits tournament players **and coaches** from officiating. This conflicts with the notes' suggestion that assistant coaches may referee.
- Submit the RRA-signed Referee Nomination with badge level, certification date, center/assistant competency by division, contact information, and whether the referee has a child on the team. Referees subsequently register online.
- **Check in at Referee HQ at least 20 minutes before the assignment**, per the webpage and updated Referee Plan.
- Wear a full referee uniform with AYSO badge. Gold is the primary color; bring an alternate for team color conflicts. Complete game cards and misconduct reports and return them to the Field Monitor immediately after the match.
- The plan provides a daily meal ticket, water/snacks, and a commemorative coin and pin for participating referees.

Youth referees need RRA authorization and must be **at least two years older than the age group they officiate**. Carry the Youth Referee Letter at all times. The letter requires a parent/guardian present or an attached Youth Volunteer Registration Form; the Referee Plan separately requires youth referees to carry their Youth Volunteer Application. Neither permits tournament players to referee; the letter now correctly names the Panther Shootout Tournament.

### Change Links in One Place

Edit the `LINKS` object near the top of `panther_shootout_main.html`:

- `url`: destination for the standalone version. Repeated buttons, text links, and the field-map image reference the same configuration entry.
- `enabled`: `true` enables a link; `false` leaves it visibly inactive in both versions.
- `sportsConnectUrl`: optional public HTTPS destination for a locally stored file. Upload the file to Sports Connect first, then paste its actual public URL here or in `url`. When this override is empty, an existing public `url` is reused; local files display as **coming soon** in the fragment.

Keep `LINKS` JSON-compatible: double-quoted keys/strings, lowercase `true`/`false`, and no trailing commas or comments inside the object.

### Generate the Sports Connect Fragment

After editing content, base CSS, or link configuration in the standalone page, run from this repository:

```powershell
python tools/build_sportsconnect.py
```

This uses Python 3's standard library and overwrites `panther_shootout_sportsconnect.html`. It copies visible content, resolves links to literal URLs, and inlines the page's simple tag/class CSS rules. Hover/focus rules and media queries are excluded; the base layout wraps without media queries. Edit the standalone source and regenerate instead of maintaining two copies manually.

Python is needed only to regenerate the fragment, not to use either HTML version. In this workspace, if `python` is unavailable, the portable runtime previously downloaded for PDF editing can run the exporter:

```powershell
& ..\.pdf-tools\python\python.exe tools/build_sportsconnect.py
```

To check synchronization and the export's link handling:

```powershell
python tools/build_sportsconnect.py --check
python -m unittest discover -s tools -p "test_*.py"
```

### Paste into Sports Connect

1. Open `panther_shootout_sportsconnect.html` in a text editor and copy its entire contents.
2. Open the destination content module's **HTML/source** editor in Sports Connect and paste the fragment there, not into the visual rich-text view.
3. Save and preview on desktop and mobile. Check that the editor retains inline styling and that the form links open correctly.
4. Once the 2026 files are uploaded, fill in their `sportsConnectUrl` values, regenerate, and paste the updated fragment.

The fragment is checked locally; its saved appearance inside your particular Sports Connect editor still needs a preview there. Local `docs/...` paths cannot serve repository files from a Sports Connect page.
