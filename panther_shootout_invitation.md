# Panther Shootout invitation email

**Subject:** You're invited! Panther Shootout • November 27–28 • Newbury Park

**Preview text:** Thanksgiving weekend soccer for AYSO boys and girls 10U, 12U and 14U. Register by November 20.

**Graphic:** Use the existing [Panther Shootout emblem](https://www.aysoregion42.org/portals/14695/docs/panthershootout/pso_emblem_2026%20(2).png) above the headline. The companion HTML file includes it at email-friendly dimensions.

---

**Make Panther Shootout part of your Thanksgiving weekend!**

Hi Coaches, Team Managers and Regional Leaders,

AYSO Region 42 invites your team to the **28th Annual Panther Shootout** in Newbury Park! Whether you joined us last year or are visiting for the first time, we'd love to welcome your players and families for two days of soccer and our signature Shootout Competition.

**When:** Friday–Saturday, November 27–28, 2026  
**Where:** Pepper Tree Playfields, 3720 Old Conejo Rd, Thousand Oaks, CA 91320  
**Divisions:** AYSO boys and girls — 10U, 12U and 14U  
**Registration deadline:** November 20, 2026  
**Rain date:** Sunday, November 29, 2026

The fun continues after each pool-play game with a separate **Shootout Competition**, with goals tallied separately from match standings and a prize for the winning team in each age group.

**Team entry fees:** 10U: $600 · 12U: $650 · 14U: $700. Each team also pays a $270 referee deposit, refundable under the tournament's referee requirements. AYSO United teams are not eligible.

**[Register your team](https://forms.gle/Ybfbc7NrieDRfQ1W9)**

Start with the online Team Registration form, then submit your signed application and arrange regional payment. **You can submit your application without waiting for your roster.** Registration starts the process; team acceptance is confirmed separately.

Find application forms, referee requirements and tournament rules at **[panthershootout.aysoregion42.org](https://panthershootout.aysoregion42.org/)**.

Regional leaders: please share this invitation with your coaches and tournament coordinators. We'd love to see your region represented!

See you on the fields,

**Alex Timen**  
Tournament Director | AYSO Region 42 Panther Shootout  
[tournament.director@aysoregion42.org](mailto:tournament.director@aysoregion42.org)
