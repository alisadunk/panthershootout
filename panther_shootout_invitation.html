Hi Coaches, Team Managers and Regional Leaders,

AYSO Region 42 invites your team to the 28th Annual Panther Shootout in Newbury Park! Whether you joined us last year or are visiting for the first time, we'd love to welcome your players and families for two days of soccer and our signature Shootout Competition.

November 27–28, 2026
Friday & Saturday · Thanksgiving weekend

Pepper Tree Playfields
3720 Old Conejo Rd, Thousand Oaks, CA 91320

AYSO boys & girls: 10U · 12U · 14U
Register by November 20
Rain date: Sunday, November 29, 2026

The fun continues after each pool-play game with a separate Shootout Competition, with goals tallied separately from match standings and a prize for the winning team in each age group.

Team entry fees
10U: $600  ·  12U: $650  ·  14U: $700
Each team also pays a $270 referee deposit, refundable under the tournament's referee requirements. AYSO United teams are not eligible.

Register your team

Start with the online Team Registration form, then submit your signed application and arrange regional payment. You can submit your application without waiting for your roster. Registration starts the process; team acceptance is confirmed separately.

Find application forms, referee requirements and tournament rules at panthershootout.aysoregion42.org.

Regional leaders: please share this invitation with your coaches and tournament coordinators. We'd love to see your region represented!
