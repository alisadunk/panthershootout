Panther Shootout – Registration & Info

Greetings

Region 42 is proud to invite AYSO boys and girls competitive teams in 10U, 12U, and 14U divisions to the 28th Annual Panther Shootout. After each pool play game, teams participate in the Shootout Competition.

The tournament is held over Thanksgiving weekend, Friday & Saturday, November 27–28, 2026. All games are held at Pepper Tree Playfields in Newbury Park: 3720 Old Conejo Rd, Thousand Oaks, CA 91320.

The best parking option is at Newbury Park High School, a short five-minute walk from the fields. Please DO NOT park in the business zone above the fields—cars are frequently towed from that area.

For Coaches

Please arrive for check-in about an hour before your first match. Bring your Game Cards, Roster, and Player Release Forms.

Five game cards must be printed using the autofill Excel template on card stock.

Every player takes part in the Shootout Competition: five kickers and one goalkeeper for each of the three shootouts after pool play games. One of the game cards is used to track participation. Refer to the Tournament Rules for full details.

For Referees

In addition to coach's submission of referee nomination form all referees must register online. A link to self-schedule will be emailed to each registered referee with additional instructions during the week of the tournament.

Please check in at the yellow pop-canopy Referee HQ at least 20 minutes before your assignment so your match is recorded and credited to your team. Stand-by referees could be asked to act as Field Monitors when needed.

Youth referees may not also be rostered players. Any referee with a child in the tournament must refrain from officiating their child’s match to maintain neutrality.

Center Referees: If a spectator, coach, or player becomes abusive or refuses to follow instructions, you are empowered to stop the match immediately by picking up the ball and directing the offending individual to leave the park. Do not restart the match until they comply. A Field Monitor will contact HQ so staff can assist.

For Volunteers

Please come and volunteer for the Panther Shootout! We need your help to make this event a success. Check in at Tournament HQ upon arrival to receive your volunteer T-shirt. Go Panthers!

  • Field Monitors: keep communication flowing between Tournament HQ, Referee HQ, Security, and EMT. In case of injury or safety concern, immediately radio Tournament HQ and report the situation. Pick up your orange vest and radio at check-in and return them at the end of your shift.
  • Field Setup: we assemble goals for 40+ visiting teams. Please bring work gloves and arrive as early as possible (around 6:00 AM is preferred). Setup should be complete by 7:30 AM.
  • Other volunteer opportunities include merchandise sales and assisting with the Shootout Competition.
  • Important Notice: Newbury Park teams are strongly encouraged to provide parent volunteers in field setup, field monitoring, and tear-down. Region 42 PSO teams that cannot provide coverage in these categories may receive a one-point deduction in pool standings to support fairness and shared participation.
