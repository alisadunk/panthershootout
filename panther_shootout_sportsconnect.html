Panther Shootout – Registration & Info

Greetings

Region 42 is proud to invite AYSO boys and girls competitive teams in 10U, 12U, and 14U divisions to the 28th Annual Panther Shootout. After each pool play game, teams participate in the Shootout Competition.

The tournament is held over Thanksgiving weekend, Friday & Saturday, November 27–28, 2026. All games are held at Pepper Tree Playfields in Newbury Park: 3720 Old Conejo Rd, Thousand Oaks, CA 91320.

We look forward to hosting several food and beverage vendors offering gourmet coffee, tacos, burgers, hot dogs, fruit cups, funnel cake, and more.

The best parking option is at Newbury Park High School, a short five-minute walk from the fields. Please DO NOT park in the business zone above the fields—cars are frequently towed from that area.

Team Registration

Coaches, let's get your team ready for kickoff! Follow these three steps to prepare your tournament application. Registration runs October 1–November 20, 2026.

Your Road to the Panther Shootout

  1. Register your team. Complete Team Registration to introduce your team to Tournament HQ and receive tournament updates.

  2. Complete the Application, get the Regional Commissioner's (RC) signature and other required approvals and forms, then upload the completed application**.

    Your roster, guest-player forms, and referee nomination* (including youth-referee forms, if applicable) can be uploaded separately before the end of registration on November 20. Please submit your application without waiting for your roster.

  3. Arrange regional payment. Contact your AYSO Region's treasurer to send payment by check or Zelle using the details below.
    • Check: Make checks payable to AYSO Region 42 / Panther Shootout.
    • Zelle: psopay@aysoregion42.org
      Message: PSO Div-Region#-CoachName
    Mail regional checks to:
    Panther Shootout
    P.O. Box 709
    Newbury Park, CA 91319-0709

* If your team does not send referees, your referee deposit will be forfeited.

** If you are unable to upload your completed forms, you may mail them to the address in step 3.

Questions: tournament.director@aysoregion42.org

For Coaches

Please arrive for check-in about an hour before your first match. Bring exactly five completed Game Cards printed on card stock using the tournament's fillable PDF template, along with your Roster and Player Release Forms.

Shootout Competition

After each pool play game, teams face the team they just played in a separate shootout area. Goals scored across the shootouts are tallied separately from match standings, and the winning team in each age group earns a prize.

Every player takes part in the Shootout Competition: five kickers and one goalkeeper for each of the three shootouts after pool play games. One of the game cards is used to track participation. Refer to the Tournament Rules for full details.

For Referees

In addition to the coach's submission of the Referee Nomination Form, all referees must register online. Referee registration and game self-scheduling will use the same third-party website.

Referee registration opens November 21, 2026. Registration opens after team registration closes so all participating teams can be listed and referees can select the team they represent. The registration and scheduling link will be available here when referee registration opens.

Please check in at the yellow pop-canopy Referee HQ at least 20 minutes before your assignment so your match is recorded and credited to your team. Stand-by referees could be asked to act as Field Monitors when needed.

Youth referees may NOT also be rostered players. Any referee with a child in the tournament must refrain from officiating their child’s match to maintain neutrality.

For Volunteers

Please come and volunteer for the Panther Shootout! We need your help to make this event a success. Check in at Tournament HQ upon arrival to receive your volunteer T-shirt. Go Panthers!

  • Field Monitors: keep communication flowing between Tournament HQ, Referee HQ, Security, and EMT. In case of injury or safety concern, immediately radio Tournament HQ and report the situation. Pick up your orange vest and radio at check-in and return them at the end of your shift.
  • Field Setup: we assemble goals for 40+ visiting teams. Please bring work gloves and arrive as early as possible (around 6:00 AM is preferred). Setup should be complete by 7:30 AM.
  • Other volunteer opportunities include merchandise sales and assisting with the Shootout Competition.
  • Important Notice: Newbury Park teams are strongly encouraged to provide parent volunteers in field setup, field monitoring, and tear-down. Region 42 PSO teams that cannot provide coverage in these categories may receive a one-point deduction in pool standings to support fairness and shared participation.
